# 30-Day AI Adoption Plan
## Week 1 — Measure
Day 1: Complete workflow audit.
Day 2: Select a low-risk workflow.
Day 3: Record baseline time, cost, and error rate.
Day 4: Review privacy/security constraints.
Day 5: Select prompt(s) and approval owner.
Days 6–7: Run five test cases.

## Week 2 — Pilot
Days 8–10: Test on non-sensitive or synthetic inputs.
Days 11–12: Revise instructions and document failures.
Days 13–14: Decide whether to proceed.

## Week 3 — Controlled rollout
Days 15–17: Use with human review on limited real tasks only if approved.
Days 18–19: Collect time and quality metrics.
Days 20–21: Review exceptions and customer feedback.

## Week 4 — Evaluate
Days 22–24: Compare metrics with baseline.
Days 25–26: Document SOP and escalation rules.
Days 27–28: Train backup reviewer.
Day 29: Decide expand / revise / stop.
Day 30: Record ROI estimate, risks, and next workflow.
