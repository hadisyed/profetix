# Profetix AI Business Starter Kit
**Version 1.0 — digital product draft | Planned price: CA$49**

A practical, editable toolkit for small businesses. No subscription or proprietary software required.

## Included
1. `business-audit.md` — identify opportunities and prioritize them.
2. `prompt-library.md` — 12 reusable prompts for everyday business tasks.
3. `30-day-action-plan.md` — launch plan with measurable milestones.

## How to use
Complete the audit, choose one repetitive workflow, and adapt the prompts using your own non-sensitive business information. Review every AI output before using it. Never paste customer personal information, passwords, financial account details, or confidential records into an AI service without appropriate authorization and safeguards.
