# Reusable Small Business AI Prompts
Replace bracketed text. Do not provide sensitive data. Verify claims, prices, and legal requirements.

1. **Offer clarity:** "Act as a small-business copy editor. Rewrite this offer for [audience] using clear benefits, scope, and exclusions: [non-sensitive offer]. Provide 3 versions. Do not invent testimonials or results."
2. **FAQ draft:** "Create 10 FAQs from these verified facts: [facts]. Flag questions that cannot be answered from the facts."
3. **Customer email:** "Draft a friendly response to this anonymized customer question: [question]. Do not promise refunds, dates, or policies not supplied."
4. **Meeting agenda:** "Create a 30-minute discovery agenda for [service] with 6 questions, desired outcomes, and a follow-up checklist."
5. **SOP outline:** "Turn these steps [steps] into a numbered standard operating procedure with owner, inputs, exceptions, and quality checks."
6. **Social calendar:** "Create a 2-week educational content calendar for [business] targeting [audience]. Avoid exaggerated claims and include a review checklist."
7. **Blog outline:** "Outline an SEO-friendly article on [topic] for [audience]. Identify factual claims requiring sources; do not fabricate citations."
8. **Proposal draft:** "Draft a proposal outline for [project] with scope, assumptions, deliverables, exclusions, milestones, and acceptance criteria. Leave prices blank."
9. **Discovery questions:** "Generate 12 questions to understand [industry] clients' current workflow, budget, decision process, and success criteria."
10. **Objection handling:** "List likely objections to [offer] and transparent, non-pushy responses. Avoid invented guarantees."
11. **Weekly review:** "Given anonymized metrics [metrics], summarize trends, caveats, and three experiments. Do not claim causality without evidence."
12. **Quality assurance:** "Review this draft [text] for ambiguity, unsupported claims, privacy risks, accessibility, and next-step clarity. Return prioritized edits."

## Universal output review
Check factual accuracy, tone, privacy, bias, accessibility, brand consistency, and human approval before publishing.
