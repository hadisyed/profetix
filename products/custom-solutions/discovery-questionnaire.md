# Client Discovery Questionnaire
Client/company: ______  Contact: ______  Date: ______

1. What business outcome do you want?
2. What does the current process look like, step by step?
3. How many times per week does it happen?
4. Who uses or approves the output?
5. What software and accounts are involved?
6. What data types are involved? Any personal, regulated, or confidential information?
7. What integrations and API permissions are available?
8. What are your current baseline costs/time/error rates?
9. What is your target budget range?
10. What timeline and key dependencies apply?
11. Who signs off on requirements and acceptance?
12. Who will own hosting, billing, credentials, and ongoing maintenance?
13. What must never be automated?
14. What does a successful pilot look like?
15. Are there procurement, accessibility, security, or legal reviews required?

**Do not request passwords or production secrets in this questionnaire.**
