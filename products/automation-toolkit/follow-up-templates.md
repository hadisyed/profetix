# Follow-Up Templates
Use only when there is a lawful basis to send the message; for commercial electronic messages, verify applicable consent and identification/unsubscribe requirements.

## Response to inbound consultation
Subject: Your Profetix inquiry
Hello [name], thanks for contacting Profetix about [specific challenge]. To recommend the right next step, could you share [one or two relevant questions]? We can then suggest a scope and estimate. Regards, Profetix — hello@profetix.com.

## Discovery recap
Subject: Next steps for [project]
Hello [name], here's our understanding: [problem], [goal], [constraints]. Proposed next step: [meeting/proposal]. Please correct anything we've misunderstood. Regards, Profetix.

## One-time check-in on an active inquiry
Subject: Following up on [project]
Hello [name], checking whether you still want to discuss [requested service]. If priorities have changed, no problem. Regards, Profetix.

## Marketing outreach
Do not send automatically. Require a documented lawful basis, correct sender identification, mailing address/contact details as applicable, and functional unsubscribe mechanism. Have a qualified reviewer approve compliance and content.
