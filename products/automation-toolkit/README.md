# Profetix AI Automation Toolkit
**Version 0.1 — implementation template, not a deployed service | Planned price: CA$199**

Includes a no-code-friendly lead intake schema, transparent scoring rules, follow-up templates, and a manual implementation checklist. Buyers must configure their own services and test them.

## Workflow
1. Prospect submits an inquiry with purpose and consent details.
2. Store minimal data in an access-controlled sheet or CRM.
3. Score business fit and inquiry urgency using `lead-scoring.md`.
4. Human reviewer checks suitability and prepares a reply.
5. Send only appropriate replies; do not assume an inquiry authorizes marketing.
6. Record outcomes and honor unsubscribe/deletion requests.

Files: `lead-intake.csv`, `lead-scoring.md`, `follow-up-templates.md`, `implementation-checklist.md`.

**Not included:** scraping, enrichment subscriptions, sending infrastructure, a live CRM integration, automated email sending, or legal compliance certification.
