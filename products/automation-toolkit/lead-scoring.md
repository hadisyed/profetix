# Transparent Lead Prioritization
This is a heuristic for **business inquiries**, not a prediction or automated decision.

## Score (0–100)
- Service fit: 0–30
- Stated project urgency: 0–20
- Budget compatibility (if voluntarily disclosed): 0–20
- Decision readiness (if voluntarily disclosed): 0–15
- Completeness of business problem: 0–15

Thresholds: 75–100 review promptly; 50–74 clarify needs; 0–49 request more information or archive. Never use protected characteristics, inferred sensitive attributes, or undisclosed personal profiling. Record why each score was assigned; allow human override.

## Signal-based prospecting extension
Potential company-level signals: a public launch announcement, hiring for administrative roles, new locations, new online services. Record URL, publication date, source credibility, relevance, and expiration. Verify manually. Do not scrape restricted sources or harvest personal emails. An external business signal is **not** consent to send commercial electronic messages; review CASL and other applicable laws.

## Quality assurance
Spot-check 10 records each week for score consistency, missing evidence, duplicate contacts, and opt-out compliance.
